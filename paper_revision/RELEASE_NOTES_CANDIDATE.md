# iotj-revision-v12 — paper revision release

Use the [numerical attachment](https://github.com/JinleLiu/CoordiWorld/releases/download/iotj-revision-v12/CoordiWorld_numerics_v12a.zip) with `scripts/download_paper_inputs.py`, then run `scripts/reproduce_paper.py --output new_saved_output` and `scripts/reproduce_figures.py --assets-dir paper_revision/numerics/paper_assets --output new_figures`. The author approved the public code/configuration list and exact numerical attachment. `ACCESS.json` records the public anonymous endpoint, file size and SHA. External verification results are recorded on the [Release page](https://github.com/JinleLiu/CoordiWorld/releases/tag/iotj-revision-v12) after publication; they are distinct from the local acceptance below.

This version corresponds to *CoordiWorld: Penalty-Coupled Utility Learning for Structured Trajectory Evaluation*. It includes the C1 method map, complete A/B source copies, frozen configurations, provenance bindings and the paper team's sole plotting implementation. Historical method entries and unchanged model bytes remain accessible. No journal acceptance, DOI or new license is asserted.

The final-layout local CPU acceptance reproduced all five frozen comparisons with rtol=0 and atol=1e-10: C1 87.21473047728695, S1 84.85050801137633, difference 2.3642224659106126, 95% CI [1.3342334425852622, 3.6479198659883108]. The numerical attachment uses stable order-preserving aliases, without changing observations, groups, missing denominators or time anchors. Plot coordinates and parameters retain the sole approved implementation; only route identifier text changes.

The existing synthetic model-integrity check and three component imports are separate from saved-result recalculation. Full original-environment inference, native scoring and training are not validated by these checks. Third-party raw assets remain subject to their providers' terms; existing notices are preserved. The internal v12 review ZIP is excluded from release assets.
